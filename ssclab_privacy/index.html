Privacy Policy – SSC Lab

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SSC Lab (“the app”), an Android app developed and published by WhiteOceanBook (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use it. It also contains our Refund Policy for PRO purchases. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • No sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email inside the app.
  • Your progress, scores, bookmarks and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob: only banner ads and rewarded interstitial ads. PRO users see no ads.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Refunds can be requested within 48 hours of purchase. After 48 hours, purchases are non-refundable.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

If you contact us by email (for support, feedback or a bug report), we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message, such as your device model or a screenshot you choose to attach. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems.

1.2 Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet so it can work properly. This data is not sent to us:

  • Quiz progress, scores, attempts and your performance graph
  • Bookmarked questions
  • Settings such as theme, dark/light mode, font, sound and vibration
  • The time you last used the app (used only to schedule practice reminders)
  • Your PRO status and expiry date

1.3 Information collected by Google services

The app uses services provided by Google. These services may collect information directly from your device under Google’s own privacy policy, as explained in sections 2, 3 and 4 below.

We do not use any analytics or tracking tools of our own.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we show ads through Google AdMob. The app uses only two ad formats:

  • Banner ads: small ads shown at the bottom of a screen.
  • Rewarded interstitial ads: a full-screen ad you can choose to watch to see your result. A notice is shown before the ad, and you can decline it.

No other ad formats are used. Ads are not shown to users with an active PRO plan.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Your device’s Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (such as device model, Android version and app version)
  • Ad interactions (such as views and taps) and diagnostic data

Google may use this information to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to prevent fraud and to measure ad performance. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool.

Control your ads: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, from your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. PRO purchases (Google Play Billing)

SSC Lab offers an optional PRO plan that removes all ads. All study content stays the same for free and PRO users.

PlanPRO duration
1 Month30 days
1 Year365 days
Lifetime3 years from the date of purchase
  • All plans are one-time purchases. They are not subscriptions and do not renew automatically.
  • The current price is always shown in the app and on the Google Play payment screen before you pay.
  • Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card, UPI, net-banking or wallet details.
  • From Google Play, the app receives only the purchase details it needs: the plan bought, the purchase time, the order status and a purchase token. These are used to activate PRO, to restore it, and to prevent misuse.
  • If you reinstall the app or move to a new device, PRO is restored automatically when you use the same Google account. You can also tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

4. Refund Policy

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: you can request a refund through Google Play. Open the Google Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, choose the SSC Lab purchase and request a refund, or use Google Play’s refund page.
  • If Google Play is unable to process your refund, email us at support@whiteoceanbook.com within 48 hours of purchase. Please include your Google Play order ID (it starts with GPA. and is in your purchase receipt email) and the email address of the Google account you used.
  • After 48 hours from the time of purchase, all purchases are non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law.
  • If a refund is issued, the PRO benefits linked to that purchase may be removed.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows occasional practice reminders. They are created and scheduled on your device. No data is sent to any server to send them. Reminders are shown only between 9 AM and 10 PM (Indian Standard Time), and not on days you have already used the app. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s Settings → Apps → SSC Lab → Notifications.

7. Other Google Play features

  • In-app updates: the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and helps you update.
  • In-app review: the app may show Google Play’s rating window. Google handles your rating and review. We cannot see who rated.
  • App backup: if Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the app’s data (such as your progress and settings) to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new device. Google manages this backup, and you can turn it off in your phone’s backup settings.
  • Crash and performance reports: Google Play may share crash and performance reports with us. These do not identify you personally, and we use them only to fix bugs.

8. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is shared only:

  • with Google, as described above, so that ads, payments, updates and reviews can work; and
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of WhiteOceanBook.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google’s policies. That is why PRO can be restored on reinstall.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google under Google’s privacy policy.

10. Children’s privacy

SSC Lab is meant for students aged 18 and above who are preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your study data stays on your device, and payments are handled through Google Play’s secure system. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect any information we handle.

12. Links to other apps and websites

The app may link to the Google Play Store, our website and our other apps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

13. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you (for example, your support emails), or ask a question about this policy. You can also control advertising and notifications from your device settings, as described above. We will reply within a reasonable time.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
