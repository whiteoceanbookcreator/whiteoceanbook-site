Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the WhiteOceanBook mobile application ("the app", "we", "our") handles information. WhiteOceanBook is an offline English vocabulary practice app for SSC, Banking and other government exam aspirants. Your privacy matters to us, and we have designed the app so that we do not collect any personal data ourselves. The free version of the app shows banner ads and interstitial (full-screen) ads provided by Google AdMob, and some question sets are locked. The optional Pro version removes all ads and unlocks every set (see the "Advertising" and "Free version, locked sets & Pro" sections below).

Free version

  • Banner ads on some screens
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads
  • Some question sets are locked

Pro version

  • No ads of any kind
  • Full access to all question sets
  • One-time purchase via Google Play

Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account, sign-up, email, or phone number is required to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, or files.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools inside the app. Google AdMob, which shows the banner and interstitial ads, may collect some device data as described in the "Advertising" section below.
  • No personal data is sent from your device to us. Optional in-app purchases are handled by Google Play (see the "In-app purchases & payments" section below), and ads are handled by Google AdMob (see the "Advertising" section below).

Data stored on your device

To make the app useful, some information is saved only on your own device (local storage):

  • Your quiz progress and accuracy for each set
  • Your bookmarked questions
  • Your app preferences (such as dark mode)
  • Whether you have unlocked Pro, and until when

This data never leaves your phone. It is not visible to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Advertising

To keep the app free, the free version shows two types of ads, both provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google:

  • Banner ads — a small ad at the bottom of some screens (not on the Home screen).
  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads that may appear at natural breaks while you use the app, for example when moving between screens. You can close an interstitial ad and continue where you left off.

How Google AdMob handles information:

  • To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as your device's Advertising ID, approximate location (based on your IP address), device and app information, interactions with ads (for example, ad views and taps), and crash and diagnostic information.
  • This information is used by Google to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. It is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never receive it in a form that identifies you.
  • Google AdMob does not receive your quiz progress, bookmarks, or any other study data from the app.
  • Ads may be personalised based on your interests. You can turn off ad personalisation or reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • If you are in a region where consent is required by law (such as the European Economic Area or the UK), the app will ask for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads.
  • Pro users see no ads. Buying any Pro plan removes all ads — both banner and interstitial — for as long as the plan is active.
  • To learn how Google uses this information, please see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Free version, locked sets & Pro

In the free version, some question sets are locked. You can still practise all the sets that are open to you without any purchase.

  • Pro gives you full access to all question sets and removes all ads.
  • Whether a set is locked or unlocked is decided on your device, based on whether Pro is active. No information about which sets you open is sent to us.
  • Locking sets does not involve collecting any extra data from you.

In-app purchases & payments

The app offers optional one-time in-app purchases (a "Pro" unlock that unlocks all question sets and removes all ads). Buying Pro is completely optional — the free version can be used without any purchase, with ads and some sets locked.

  • All payments are processed securely by Google Play. We never see, receive, or store your card, bank, UPI, or any other payment details.
  • WhiteOceanBook does not collect or store any of your payment or billing information.
  • Your Pro unlock is recorded only on your own device to enable Pro features. Google Play holds the official record of your purchase.
  • Purchases are one-time (not auto-renewing) — nothing is ever charged automatically.
  • When you make a purchase, Google processes the transaction under the Google Privacy Policy. Refunds and payment support are handled by Google Play.

Refund policy

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If the Pro unlock is not what you expected, you may request a refund within 48 hours of the payment.

  • Refund requests raised within 48 hours of the transaction will be honoured.
  • You can request the refund directly through Google Play, or write to us at support@whiteoceanbook.com with your Google Play order ID, and we will help you process it.
  • After 48 hours, refunds are handled at the discretion of Google Play under its standard refund policy.
  • Once a refund is issued, Pro access is removed and the app returns to its free version (with ads and some sets locked).

Restoring your unlimited access

Because your Pro unlock is stored only on your device, your unlimited access will be removed from that device if you:

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Clear the app's data from your phone's settings

This does not mean you have lost what you paid for. Google Play keeps the official record of your purchase. Until your access is restored, the app on that device works as the free version (with ads and some sets locked).

  • To get your unlimited access back, write to support@whiteoceanbook.com and claim it. Please send the message from the email address linked to your Google Play account and include your Google Play order ID if you have it.
  • We will verify your purchase and restore your unlimited access.
  • There is no extra charge for restoring access — you never have to pay twice for the same purchase.
  • Please note that quiz progress and bookmarks cannot be restored, as that data is stored only on your device and we keep no copy of it.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage files. All study features work fully offline. To show banner and interstitial ads in the free version, the app uses an internet connection and the device's Advertising ID (permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), which is used only by Google AdMob as described in the "Advertising" section.

Third-party services

The app uses only these third-party services:

  • Google Play — processes optional in-app purchases, as described above.
  • Google AdMob — shows banner and interstitial ads in the free version, as described in the "Advertising" section.

No other third-party services that collect data are used. Question content, progress tracking, and all study features run locally on your device.

Children's privacy

The app is an educational tool suitable for students and the general public. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We collect no data ourselves; the banner and interstitial ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob under Google's policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe otherwise, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Data security

Because your data (progress, bookmarks, settings) is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us, there is no server-side data held by us that could be exposed. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data handled by Google AdMob, as described in the "Advertising" section. We additionally follow Android best practices, such as disabling app data backup extraction, to keep your local data private to the app.

Data deletion

You are in full control of your data. All app data (progress, bookmarks, settings) is automatically and permanently deleted when you:

  • Uninstall the app from your device, or
  • Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → WhiteOceanBook → Storage → Clear Data (App info)

No request to us is needed, because we hold no copy of your data on any server. Records of any purchases are kept by Google Play and can be reviewed in your Google account. If you had purchased Pro, please see the "Restoring your unlimited access" section above before deleting your data.

External links

The app or our website may contain links to external sites (for example, the Google Play Store page or our website). This policy applies only to the WhiteOceanBook app. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if new features are added. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
