FrogVocab is a vocabulary game for English learners and exam aspirants. You tap the frog to catch bees, and each bee brings a word of easy, medium or tough level whose meaning is shown on screen. The app covers topics such as One Word Substitution, Synonyms & Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Root Words and Phrasal Verbs, and lets you save words, earn coins and unlock new frog designs.
