FrogVocab – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Developer: WhiteOceanBook

This Privacy Policy explains how FrogVocab (the "App"), an English vocabulary game for Android published by WhiteOceanBook ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

In short:
  • No sign-up, no login, and no account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress, coins, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your data.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and the Pro subscription is handled by Google Play. These Google services process some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following on your phone or tablet:

  • Words you have already seen, and your position in the word lists
  • Coins earned, frogs unlocked and the frog you selected
  • Words you have bookmarked
  • Settings such as theme (light/dark), sounds and reminders
  • Whether your Pro subscription is active

This information is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms. You can manage this in your phone's backup settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, after every few words. Pro users do not see ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown, watched or clicked)

Google may use this information to deliver, personalise and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form so that you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

More information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Ad Settings

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

FrogVocab offers an optional Pro monthly subscription, which removes ads and unlocks all frogs. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

The App receives only the information it needs from Google Play to confirm your subscription, such as the product ID, purchase status and a purchase token. Because the subscription is linked to your Google account, Pro can be restored after reinstalling the App on the same Google account.

You can cancel at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to all payments.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may show a friendly reminder when you have not played for a few hours. These reminders are scheduled locally on your device; they are not sent from a server, and no notification data is collected. You can turn reminders off in the App's Settings or in your Android notification settings at any time.

5. Permissions used by the App

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Apart from the Google services described above (AdMob and Google Play), the App does not share information with any third party. We do not use any separate analytics or tracking tools.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Doing either deletes it.
  • Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies. You can manage it from your Google account.
  • Because we do not hold any of your personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have any request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

FrogVocab is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and want help, please contact us.

9. Security

Game data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Payments and ads are handled by Google over secure connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please check this page periodically.
