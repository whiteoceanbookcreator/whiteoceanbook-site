1. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following only in its private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Your progress in each topic (One Word Substitution, Synonyms + Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Root Words and Phrasal Verbs) and in each level (Easy, Medium, Tough)
  • The current round, so you can continue from the same point after closing the App
  • Coins earned, frogs unlocked and the frog you are playing with
  • Words you have saved (bookmarked)
  • Settings: Light or Dark theme, sounds and background music
  • Your Pro status and the date it ends
  • Small app notes, such as whether we have already asked for notification permission or for a rating

This information is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms. You can manage this in your phone's backup settings.

The App's sounds and music play from files inside the App. The App never records audio and does not use the microphone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob:

  • A full-screen ad may appear at a natural break: after you finish a round of 10 words and tap Continue.
  • A banner ad may appear at the bottom of the Saved Words screen.

Users with an active Pro plan do not see ads. When your device is offline, the App shows its own short "Remove ads? Get PRO" card instead of an ad. No data is collected for this card.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version and app version
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location
  • Ad interactions, for example whether an ad was shown or clicked

Google may use this information to deliver, personalise and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3. Pro purchases (Google Play)

FrogVocab offers optional Pro plans. Each plan is a one-time payment. It is not a subscription and nothing renews or charges you again automatically.

PlanWhat you getHow long
1 MonthNo ads30 days from purchase
LifetimeNo ads + every frog unlocked3 years from purchase

Frogs you unlocked with coins stay yours even after a Pro plan ends. Frogs unlocked only by the Lifetime plan lock again when it ends.

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. The App receives from Google Play only what it needs to confirm a purchase: the product ID, purchase status, purchase time and a purchase token. Because the purchase is linked to your Google account, Pro can be restored after you reinstall the App using the same Google account. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to all payments.

4. Refund policy

If you bought a Pro plan by mistake or are not happy with it, you can ask for a refund within 48 hours of purchase, in either of these two ways:

Option A: Through Google Play

  1. Open your Google Play order history and sign in with the Google account you used to buy Pro.
  2. Find the FrogVocab Pro order.
  3. Choose Request a refund (or Report a problem) and follow the steps.

You can also open the Google Play Store app → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Option B: Email us

Write to support@whiteoceanbook.com with the subject FrogVocab Pro refund and include:

  • Your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt email)
  • The Google account email you used to buy
  • The plan you bought (1 Month or Lifetime)
  • The reason for the refund (optional, but it helps us improve)

We will check the order and, if it is within 48 hours of purchase, ask Google Play to refund it.

  • Refunds are paid by Google Play to your original payment method. The time it takes to show in your account depends on your bank or payment method.
  • When a purchase is refunded, it is cancelled and the Pro benefits from that purchase are no longer valid.
  • After 48 hours, refund requests are handled according to Google Play's refund policy, and as required by applicable law.

5. Reminder notifications

The App asks before sending notifications. If you allow them, the App may show a friendly reminder when you have not opened it for about 5 hours. These reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server, and no notification data is collected.

You can turn reminders off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → FrogVocab → Notifications.

6. Other Google Play features

  • Rating request: After you have learned some words, the App may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Google Play shows the card and handles your rating and review. We do not receive your name or account details from it.
  • App updates: About once a day, the App may ask Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update.

7. When you email us

The Help button in Settings opens your own email app with our address and a subject already filled in. The draft also includes your Android version to help us solve problems. You can see and change everything before you send it.

If you email us, for help or for a refund, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to handle your request. We do not add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

8. WhiteOceanBook app button

The App has a Visit WhiteOceanBook button for our other learning app. To make this button work, the App checks on your device whether the WhiteOceanBook app is installed. If it is, the button opens it. If not, the button opens its Google Play page. This check stays on your device and is not sent to us.

10. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Apart from the Google services described above (AdMob and Google Play), the App does not share information with any third party. We do not use any separate analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Doing either deletes it.
  • Pro purchases are kept by Google Play in your Google account, so they can be restored later.
  • Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies. You can manage it from your Google account.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you or handle your refund. Ask us and we will delete them.

12. Children's privacy

FrogVocab is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and want help, please contact us.

13. Security

Game data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Payments and ads are handled by Google over secure connections.
